Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you log in using Discord, this application receives basic user profile data (such as your Discord User ID and username) directly through Discord OAuth2.

2. How Data is Used

This application operates as a client-side tool. Your data is used solely to display your profile and manage event features within your browser session. We do not sell, share, or monetize your personal data.

3. Storage

We do not store your private credentials or personal data on external tracking servers. Any temporary session state is kept locally in your browser.

4. Third-Party Services

Authentication is handled directly by Discord. Please refer to Discord’s Privacy Policy for information on how Discord manages your account data.
